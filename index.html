Beamr: Smart TV Remote

Beamr turns your iPhone into a simple remote for your smart TV. It has no ads and no account.

Get help

Open an issue at github.com/gurkiratbola/beamr-site/issues. Say which TV brand and model you have, and what the app shows.

Quick fixes

  • Put your iPhone and your TV on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Allow Local Network access for Beamr in iPhone Settings.
  • Accept the prompt or type the code that your TV shows the first time.
  • If the TV address changed, remove the TV in the app and add it again.
