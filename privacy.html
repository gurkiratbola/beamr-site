Beamr privacy policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

Beamr is an iPhone app that controls a smart TV on your local Wi-Fi network. This policy says what the app does with your data.

Summary

Beamr does not collect your personal data. Beamr has no account, no ads and no analytics. Beamr does not send data to the developer.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • The list of TVs you save: the name, the brand, the model and the IP address of each TV.
  • A pairing token from your TV, if the TV gives one. Beamr keeps the token in the iOS Keychain.
  • Your settings, for example which TV is active.

This data stays on your iPhone. If you delete the app, iOS deletes this data. Keychain items can stay on the device after you delete an app. Remove a TV in the app to delete its token.

Local Network access

Beamr asks for Local Network permission. It uses the permission to find your TV and to send commands to your TV on your home Wi-Fi network. Beamr does not scan networks outside your own.

Purchases

Beamr Pro is an in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment. Beamr does not see your payment details. Beamr checks with Apple that you own Pro.

Third parties

Beamr uses no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. Beamr does not share data with third parties.

Children

Beamr does not ask for personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text appears at this address with a new date.
