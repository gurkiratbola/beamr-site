Beamr privacy policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

Beamr is an iPhone app that controls a smart TV on your local Wi-Fi network. This policy says what the app does with your data.

Summary

Beamr does not collect your personal data. Beamr has no account, no ads and no analytics. Beamr does not send data to the developer.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • The list of TVs you save: the name, the brand and the IP address of each TV.
  • A pairing token from your TV, if the TV gives one (Samsung). Beamr keeps the token in the iOS Keychain. If the Keychain refuses to save it, the token stays on your iPhone in the app's own storage, so you do not need to pair again.
  • For Google TV, a client certificate that Beamr creates on your iPhone, and the public key of each paired TV. Beamr keeps them in the iOS Keychain.
  • A hardware ID for each saved TV. It is a number that the TV reports about itself, for example a device ID or a Wi-Fi address. Beamr uses it to know that a TV is the same TV after its IP address changes. It stays on your iPhone. Beamr never sends it to anyone. It is not in the diagnostics text.
  • Your settings, for example which TV is active.
  • Simple counters on your iPhone: how many connects worked or failed, the kind of failure, and how many keys you pressed. Beamr never sends them. The "Copy diagnostics" button copies a short text (app version, iPhone model, iOS version, the brand, the last error and these counters) to your clipboard only when you tap it. The text has no IP address and no TV name.
  • Rating counters: how many real key presses you made, how many days you used the app, the last day you used it, and the last app version for which Beamr asked for a rating. Beamr uses them to decide when to ask for a rating. They stay on your iPhone.

This data stays on your iPhone. If you delete the app, iOS deletes this data. Keychain items can stay on the device after you delete an app. Remove a TV in the app to delete its saved token. The Google TV certificate stays until you delete the app.

Local Network access

Beamr asks for Local Network permission. It uses the permission to find your TV and to send commands to your TV on your home Wi-Fi network. Beamr does not scan networks outside your own. When a saved TV does not answer, Beamr can search your home Wi-Fi network for that TV, because its IP address may have changed. It does this only when the saved address of the TV is on the network that your iPhone uses now. It looks only at your own local network, and only at the ports of that TV brand. Beamr does not store the other devices that it finds.

Keyboard and voice

Text that you type in the Beamr keyboard goes only to your TV on your home Wi-Fi network. Beamr does not save it.

Voice commands (Beamr Pro) turn your speech into text on your iPhone. Beamr uses on-device speech recognition only. Your audio never leaves your iPhone. If your language has no on-device model on your iPhone, Beamr turns the microphone button off and does not send audio to a server. Beamr listens only while you hold the microphone button. Beamr keeps no audio and no transcript.

The dictation key of the iOS keyboard is an Apple feature. Apple's terms and Apple's privacy policy apply to it. Beamr does not receive what you dictate until the text is in the Beamr text field, and then Beamr sends it only to your TV.

Purchases

Beamr Pro is an in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment. Beamr does not see your payment details. Beamr checks with Apple that you own Pro.

Third parties

Beamr uses no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. Beamr does not share data with third parties.

Children

Beamr does not ask for personal data from anyone, including children.
